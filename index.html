Step 1 — Finding your target company

🔍

Finding Your Target Company

Search MarketLine for a company you'd consider for a co-op

Pick a company you're genuinely curious about — ideally one you could see yourself applying to for a co-op. The more specific your target, the more useful your notes will be later in this tutorial.

How to search for a company on MarketLine
  1. 1
    From the MarketLine Advantage homepage, click the Companies tab, or type the company name directly into the main search bar.
  2. 2
    Use the company's official name for the best results. MarketLine auto-suggests matches as you type.
  3. 3
    Choose the correct entry from the results list — check the headquarters country and industry to make sure it's the right one, since some companies share similar names.
  4. 4
    Click the company name to open its full profile page.
Subsidiaries and parent companies If the company you want is part of a larger corporation, try searching the parent company name too. Some subsidiaries have their own profile — others don't.

Reflection questions

Be specific: name the company and describe what draws you to it — its industry, mission, reputation, or a product you use.

Think about the title, team, or department that interests you — this will guide how you read the rest of the profile.

Step 2 — Reading the company profile

📊

Reading the Company Profile

Get to know what this company actually does

Once you open a company profile on MarketLine, you'll find a structured overview of the organization. Read it closely — this is the material you'll draw on for the rest of the tutorial.

What to look for on the profile page
  1. 1
    Company overview & description: a professionally written summary of what the company does, where it operates, and its core business.
  2. 2
    Key facts: employee count, annual revenue, and headquarters location. A company with 500 employees feels very different from one with 50,000 — size shapes culture and career paths.
  3. 3
    Business segments & products: most companies are divided into business units or product lines. If you want to work in a specific division, reference it by name in your application.
Career tip Pay attention to how MarketLine describes the company's core value proposition. If the description mentions "innovation" or "sustainability," those words are worth echoing (honestly) in your own materials later.

Reflection questions

Use what you read in the MarketLine description, but rephrase it in your own voice.

Think about what it means to be employee #200 versus employee #20,000.

Look at the segment breakdown in the profile and name the one closest to your interests.

Step 3 — What is this company looking for?

🧭

What Is This Company Looking For?

Turn your research into a list of in-demand skills

Every company profile hints at what it needs next. A growing business segment usually needs more people. A stated opportunity needs someone to chase it. An industry-wide trend needs someone who understands it. Reading between the lines here is what separates a strong co-op candidate from an average one.

Where to look for hiring clues
  1. 1
    SWOT tab → Opportunities: growth areas a company is investing in usually mean it's hiring for related skills.
  2. 2
    Business segments: whichever segment is growing fastest is likely adding the most roles.
  3. 3
    Industry tab → Key Trends: sector-wide trends (like AI adoption or new sustainability rules) point to skills that every company in that space wants.
Company profile → Opportunities & segments → Industry trends → Skills in demand

Reflection questions

Think about technical skills, domain knowledge, or analytical abilities implied by what you read.

Name a class, project, club, or job — not just the skill itself.

Step 4 — Finding a co-op position

💼

Find a Co-op Position

Turn your research into an actual application

Now that you understand the company and what it's likely looking for, it's time to check whether they're currently recruiting Northeastern co-ops.

Where to search
  • 🔎
    Search NUworks (opens in a new tab) — Northeastern's co-op and job search platform. Search the company's name, or browse by industry or major, to see whether they're currently recruiting Northeastern co-ops.
  • 🌐
    The company's own careers page: search "[Company name] careers" or "[Company name] students" or "early careers." Not every employer posts every opening to NUworks, especially smaller or newer co-op partners.
Nothing posted right now? Co-op postings are seasonal. If you don't see an open role, note that below and name one comparable company or role you'd target instead — or plan to check back before the next co-op application deadline. Your co-op or career advisor can also help you find openings that aren't posted publicly.

Reflection questions

List as many as you find — you can compare them later.

If nothing is posted, imagine the skill list a similar posting would likely include.
