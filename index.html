Step 1 — Choose what to research

🔍

Choose What to Research

Look up the company or the industry from your interview

Think back to your informational interview. You can research either the specific company your contact works for, or the broader industry they work in — whichever will teach you more.

Which should you pick? If your contact's employer is a company you can find by name, search Companies. If their employer is small, private, a nonprofit, or a government office — or you'd rather look at the field broadly — search Industries instead.
Company: how to open a company profile
  1. 1
    Click the Companies tab in the top navigation menu.
  2. 2
    Type your contact's employer into the search panel using its official name (for example, "PepsiCo, Inc." rather than "Pepsi"), then click Search.
  3. 3
    Select the correct company from the results list to open its full Company Profile.
Industry: how to open an industry profile
  1. 1
    Click the Industries tab in the top navigation menu.
  2. 2
    Type the sector your contact works in into the search panel (for example, "Commercial Banking" or "Apparel Retail"), then click Search.
  3. 3
    Select the closest match from the results list to open the full Industry Profile.

Step 2 — Research and reflect

🧭

Research & Reflect

Turn what you find into a clearer picture of this field

Spend a few minutes in MarketLine, then answer the questions below based on what you actually find — not guesses.

Where to find it on MarketLine
  1. 1
    Overview: on a Company Profile, read the description at the top of the page. On an Industry Profile, read the Market Overview. This tells you what the organization or sector actually does.
  2. 2
    SWOT Analysis (company) or Key Trends (industry): on a Company Profile, click SWOT Analysis in the left-hand menu (or the link below Key Stats) and read the Opportunities. On an Industry Profile, click Key Trends instead. Both show where the field is headed.
  3. 3
    Business Segments (company) or Competitive Landscape (industry): on a Company Profile, scroll to Business Segments / Products & Services. On an Industry Profile, click Competitive Landscape. Both hint at the skills and experience the field values.
